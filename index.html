05 / Community

By students.
For students.

Vidyarthi Seva Sangathan (VSS) is an independent, unofficial student community based around the CGIT Boys Hostel — built by students to help students. As Head and Spokesperson, I help bring students together around welfare, constructive dialogue, events and campus initiatives.

Our work has included hostel debates, esports tournaments, student workshops and community campaigns, with a growing core team and wider student support.

20+ core members
Support from 200+ students

4 hostel esports tournaments
2 hostel debate programmes

VSS is independent and unofficial, and is not affiliated with college authorities or any government body.
